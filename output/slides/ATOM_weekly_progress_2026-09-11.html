Execution sequence

Four motions, checked stage by stage

01Move to pick

MoveGroup pose goal from the current state. Skip this motion if already within the pick tolerance.

02Vertical lift

After simulated grasp success, raise the tip 0.050 m along +Z while constraining orientation.

03Constrained transfer

Cartesian motion to 0.080 m above place. This segment rises by 0.030 m as it moves across.

04Vertical descent

Descend 0.080 m along −Z to place after the alignment check. Verify the final pose.

Pick → simulated grasp confirmation → lift → transfer → descend → COMPLETE

Stages 2–4 use GetCartesianPath followed by ExecuteTrajectory. This is a one-shot sequence; it does not yet include release, retreat or return-home motions.

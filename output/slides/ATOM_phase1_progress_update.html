Current progress

Simulation foundation established

ROS 2 simulation environment

ROS 2 Jazzy, Gazebo Harmonic, ros2_control and MoveIt 2 are configured in Docker.

xArm 850 model integrated

The official URDF/Xacro model, six-joint description and TF chain are loaded.

Motion planning and execution

MoveIt plans target motions and sends the trajectory to the simulated arm controller.

Nominal dynamics-based control

A separate torque-driven simulation module records joint tracking performance.
